OutExaRD studies the problem of resource discovery in distributed exascale environments when dynamic events change system demand and resource availability.

### Key Contributions

- Introduced a **wave-vector-based** representation for connecting resource discovery to dynamically changing system activities.
- Investigated adaptive search-space generation and resource selection under interactive events.
- Reported improved handling of dynamic resource-discovery events in the paper's evaluation.
