Auto-Healer introduces a self-healing hardware architecture designed to improve the reliability and fault tolerance of autonomous driving systems, particularly their CNN-based perception workloads.

The architecture combines **chiplet-based hardware organization, FPGA reconfigurability, Dual Modular Redundancy (DMR), fault detection, and checkpoint-assisted recovery** to mitigate transient and permanent faults while preserving real-time operation.

### Key Contributions

- **Fault-tolerant architecture:** A chiplet-inspired FPGA architecture supporting automated fault detection and recovery in CNN-based perception.
- **Transient fault recovery:** Mean Time to Repair (MTTR) of 40 ns.
- **Permanent fault recovery:** Mean Time to Repair (MTTR) of 120 ns.
- **Recovery mechanisms:** Hardware redundancy, synchronization, checkpointing, and FPGA-assisted recovery.
- **Application:** Reliability and fault tolerance for autonomous driving perception systems.
