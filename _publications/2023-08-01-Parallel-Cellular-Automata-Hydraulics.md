This research develops a parallel computational architecture for accelerating hydraulic analysis of water distribution networks through **cellular automata (CA)** and parallel equation solving.

### Key Contributions

- Partitioned water distribution networks into smaller computational units suitable for parallel execution.
- Applied cellular-automata principles to the numerical solution of hydraulic-network equations.
- Evaluated the architecture as a means of accelerating computationally intensive engineering simulation workloads.

**Authors:** Ali Suvizi, Azim Farghadan, and Morteza Saheb Zamani.

**Publication:** Journal of Parallel and Distributed Computing, vol. 178, pp. 11-28, 2023.
