**Status: Preprint. Submitted to IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing (TDSC); not a peer-reviewed TDSC publication.**

Emulated Integrity Replica (EIR) explores fault detection and recovery for FPGA SoCs using **hierarchical digital twins** that take advantage of processing-system resources during accelerator execution.

### Key Contributions

- Introduced complementary **Rabbit** (coarse-grained behavioral) and **Tortoise** (fine-grained) twins for detection and recovery.
- Investigated checkpoint-assisted recovery without proportional duplication of FPGA accelerator hardware.
- Evaluated the tradeoffs among fault coverage, hardware area, energy, and recovery overhead on representative workloads.

**Authors:** Arsalan Ali Malik, Ali Suvizi, Guru Venkataramani, and Aydin Aysu.

**Preprint:** arXiv:2607.12298, 2026.
