This paper examines the tradeoffs between **Age of Information (AoI)**, task latency, and deadline compliance in chiplet-based hardware systems. Using an autonomous-driving-inspired workload, it evaluates the effects of inter-chiplet communication and Network-on-Interposer latency on scheduling decisions.

### Key Contributions

- Studied how task scheduling choices affect both data freshness and end-to-end latency.
- Explored **FD-Hybrid**, a tunable scheduling strategy balancing freshness and task deadlines.
- Evaluated scheduling behavior under chiplet communication delays and real-time constraints.
