This paper investigates energy-efficient parallel computing methods for solving nonlinear hydraulic equations in water distribution networks.

### Key Contributions

- Combined **cellular automata (CA)** with **learning automata (LA)** to support parallel hydraulic analysis.
- Applied a Taylor-series-based solution approach to reduce the computational cost of water-network analysis.
- Reported up to **60x speedup over EPANET** in the evaluated cases, with reduced energy consumption.

**Authors:** Ali Suvizi, Ruhollah Ahmadi, Morteza Saheb Zamani, and Mohammad Reza Meybodi.

**Publication:** Sustainable Computing: Informatics and Systems, vol. 46, article 101135, 2025.
